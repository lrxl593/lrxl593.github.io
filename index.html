About Me

My general interests include natural language processing, dialogue and interactive systems, large language models, and AI governance/ethics. I am particularly interested in the following fields:

  • Dialogue Systems (with emotion, cognition & behavior modeling; for mental health care applications)

  • Natural language generation theories, models & evaluation

  • AI alignment, safety and ethics

  • Social intelligence with large language models (emotion, theory-of-mind, cognition, etc.)

  • Fundamental problems in large language models and multi-modal foundation models (RL learning, RSI)

I am leading a research group named Conversational AI (CoAI, GitHub link). This group published more than 100 papers on ACL, ICLR, ICML, Neurips conferences, and has won many paper awards including COLING 2010 best paper, ACL 2012 best student paper, IJCAI 2018 distinguished paper, SIGDIAL 2020 best paper, NLPCC 2015/2020 best paper, ACL 2023/2024 area chair award, ACL 2026 SAC highlights, etc. I published highly cited works including ATAE-LSTM (second-mostly-cited paper of EMNLP 2016), ECM (emotional chatting machine, AAAI 2018), CCM (Commonsense-aware conversation model, IJCAI-2018 distinguished papers), ESC (Emotional support conversation, ACL 2021), PPT (Pretrained prompt tuning, ACL 2022), MiniLLM (ICLR 2024), ChatGLM (2024, with Prof. Jie Tang), etc.

My research is recognized by the Qian Weichang Science and Technology Award (Technological Invention, First Prize, CIPS, 2024), the Wu Wenjun AI Science and Technology Award (Science and Technology Progress, First Prize, CAAI, 2019), the Hanwang Youth Innovation Award (CIPS, 2018), and the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars (NSFC, 2022).

I have been serving as Program Co-Chair of COLING 2027 and Associate Program Chair of AAAI 2027. I have served as an Executive Committee Member at Large of AFNLP (Asian Federation of Natural Language Processing), Diversity and Inclusion Co-Chair of ACL-IJCNLP 2021, Workshop Co-Chair of EMNLP 2021, Senior Area Chair for ACL and EMNLP more than ten times, and Senior PC or Area Chair for AAAI and IJCAI. I was a member of the EMNLP 2025 Best Paper Selection Committee and of the Computational Linguistics PhD Dissertation Award Selection Committee (2025-2026). I am an Action Editor of Transactions of the ACL and of Computational Linguistics, and an Associate Editor of IEEE TNNLS and IEEE Transactions on Big Data.

I also welcome talented undergraduates and interns to join us for doing interesting and amazing research. I have supervised and led many undergraduates to do research, many of whom published top-tier conference papers during their early career, such as ACL, EMNLP, ICLR, etc. Here are my thoughts and experiences on how undergraduates can make successful research (in Chinese).
